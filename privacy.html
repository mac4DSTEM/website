Privacy policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

mac4DSTEM and macTEM collect no personal data. Both applications perform all analysis on your own machine, have no user accounts, and send nothing to me. macTEM makes no network connection at all; mac4DSTEM's one network request is a Materials Project fetch that you start yourself.

Both applications

mac4DSTEM and macTEM are local desktop applications. Neither contains analytics, telemetry, advertising, crash reporting, or any other component that transmits information about you or your work.

  • Your datasets and results stay on your Mac. Datasets you open, the results you compute, and the parameters you choose are read from and written to your own storage only.
  • No accounts. There is no sign-in, no licence server, and no activation.
  • No tracking identifiers. Neither application generates or stores a device or user identifier.

Both applications run inside the macOS App Sandbox. They receive access to a file only when you select it in a system Open or Save panel. Where one needs to reopen a file you chose earlier — for example to update the session file saved next to your data — macOS stores a security-scoped bookmark for that specific file on your machine. Those bookmarks stay local and can be removed by deleting the application's container.

mac4DSTEM

  • One network request, started by you. “Materials Project…” in the phase picker sends an HTTPS request to api.materialsproject.org carrying your own Materials Project API key and the material ID you typed. The key is kept in the macOS Keychain. The application makes no other connection.

macTEM

  • No network access. macTEM is not granted network access by the App Sandbox and makes no connection of any kind.

This website

The site is a static page. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and embeds no third-party fonts, scripts, or trackers. Nothing you do here is profiled or recorded by me.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, with DNS provided by Cloudflare. As a technical necessity of delivering any web page, the hosting provider processes your IP address and request metadata in server logs for security and operation. I have no access to those logs and cannot connect them to you. See the GitHub Privacy Statement and the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

The legal basis for this processing is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR — the legitimate interest in presenting a functioning, secure website.

If you email me

Writing to mail@mac4dstem.com means your address and the content of your message are processed so that I can reply. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where your message concerns the application, and otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Correspondence is kept only as long as needed to deal with the matter and to meet any retention obligation that applies, then deleted. Your address is not added to any mailing list and is not passed to third parties.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your personal data, to have it corrected or erased, to have its processing restricted, to object to processing, and to receive it in a portable form. To exercise any of these, write to the address below.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the member state of your residence, workplace, or the place of the alleged infringement.
