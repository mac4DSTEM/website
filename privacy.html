Privacy

mac4DSTEM and macEM collect no data. Everything runs on your Mac. There are no accounts, no analytics, and nothing is sent to me. The one exception is the Materials Project lookup in mac4DSTEM, which you start yourself and which sends your own API key and the material ID to materialsproject.org.

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps the usual server logs (GitHub privacy statement). If you e-mail me, I keep your message only to reply. Under the GDPR you can ask me to access, correct or delete your data at any time.
