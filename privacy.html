Privacy

mac4DSTEM and macTEM collect no data. Everything runs on your Mac. There are no accounts, no analytics, and nothing is sent to me. The one exception is the Materials Project lookup in mac4DSTEM, which you start yourself and which sends your own API key and the material ID to materialsproject.org.

macEM, and mac4DSTEM from version 5.3.0, have an optional door for AI assistants you run on your own Mac (Settings > Agents). It is off until you turn it on. It listens on your Mac only, answers only the assistant you give its token to, and lets that assistant work with the open windows. The app itself sends nothing anywhere. What the assistant does with what it sees, for example sending it to its own provider, is governed by that assistant's own privacy terms.

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps the usual server logs (GitHub privacy statement). If you e-mail me, I keep your message only to reply. Under the GDPR you can ask me to access, correct or delete your data at any time.
