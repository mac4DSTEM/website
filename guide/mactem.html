macTEM

Preview. macTEM is not released yet. This page lists its rooms; workflows will follow with the release.

macTEM is a native Mac app for TEM work: images, diffraction patterns, and EDS and EELS spectrum images. It uses the same window and controls as mac4DSTEM.

Rooms

RoomWhat it is for
ImageView and measure TEM images with scale bar, contrast and distance, angle and profile marks.
FFTLive Fourier transform of the image: find lattice spots and read their spacings.
DiffractionTreat an image as a diffraction pattern, calibrate on a known ring and index spots against a crystal.
EDSElement ID, maps, spectra and composition from spectrum images.
EELSLow-loss and core-loss spectra: thickness and composition.
ParticlesCount and measure particles in an image.
OutputCollect and export what you made.

The EDS, EELS and Particles rooms are still being finished.
