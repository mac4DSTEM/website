Get started

Install

mac4DSTEM is one app with everything inside. There is no Python, no package manager and nothing else to set up.

What you need

  • A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later).
  • macOS 26 or later. For macOS 14 to 25, version 3.0.0 remains available.
  • Enough memory for your data. A dataset that does not fit can still be opened with a crop or binning, or streamed from disk; see Open your data.

Download and install

  1. Download mac4DSTEM.dmg.
  2. Open the disk image and drag mac4DSTEM to your Applications folder.
  3. Open mac4DSTEM from Applications. The app is signed and notarized, so it opens without a security warning.

The welcome window offers Open Dataset…, Open with Options… and Try Demo Data. The demo is a small synthetic 4D-STEM dataset in which every workspace works. Nothing on disk is touched.

Update

Download the new disk image and drag the app to Applications again, replacing the old one. Your saved sessions live beside your datasets, not inside the app, so they are kept. All versions and what changed in each are on the releases page.

Quick Look

Once the app is installed, Finder shows thumbnails of 4D-STEM HDF5 files, and pressing the space bar on one shows a preview.

Remove

Drag mac4DSTEM from Applications to the Trash. Session files beside your datasets (they end in .mac4dstem.h5) stay where they are; delete them yourself if you no longer want them.

Privacy

No account, no analytics, no telemetry. Your data is read from and written to your own storage only. Details are in the privacy policy.
