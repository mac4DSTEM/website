macTEM

TEM images, spectra and diffraction, native on Apple silicon.

A macOS application for transmission electron microscopy: calibrated images and their Fourier transforms, EDS and EELS spectrum images taken to composition and thickness, and electron diffraction patterns calibrated and indexed. The second app of the macEM suite, beside mac4DSTEM.

Rooms

  • Image

    DigitalMicrograph DM3/DM4, TIFF (including multi-frame stacks), MRC and Velox EMD open at their calibration, several to a window. Contrast, colormap and gamma on a histogram of the raw counts; distance, angle and line-profile measurements; the acquisition metadata; particle counts and sizes.

  • FFT

    The live transform of a region with a window before it, the radial profile, spot d-spacings and Find Spots for the strongest reflections, a masked inverse, and HRTEM filters.

  • EDS

    Velox and GMS spectrum images with their HAADF. Auto ID proposes the elements by the published AutoEMX/NIST identification method; Cliff-Lorimer, zeta-factor and cross-section quantification with absorption correction; net-count, wt%, at% and thickness maps; regions drawn on the map and compared side by side.

  • EELS

    GMS spectrum images with their low loss and core loss. Zero-loss alignment, t/λ maps by the log-ratio method, and edge quantification with a power-law background, each intensity with its uncertainty: 383 K, L and M edges across 95 elements from the dft GOS set.

  • Diffraction

    Any image read as a pattern: the centre, a calibration from a ring of Au, Al or Si or from the file's own axis, fitted rings and refined spots, indexed against a standard or an imported CIF.

  • Results and export

    Every measurement in one ledger. Tables as CSV, spectra as EMSA/MSA, maps as PNG with their scale bar, and the method as JSON; marks are kept in a session file beside the image.

Provenance and validation

The numerics live in open Swift packages, each ported from and tested against the Python package the field already uses: file readers against RosettaSciIO, image numerics against NumPy, SciPy and scikit-image, crystallography against pymatgen, and spectroscopy against HyperSpy and exSpy. See the kits.

EELS cross sections are the generalised oscillator strengths of L. Segger, G. Guzzinati and H. Kohl, dft GOS v1.5.0 (CC BY 4.0), the tables exSpy uses, bundled unchanged.

Readers, indices and metadata are tested exact; numbers agree with the reference within that package's own tolerance. Where a result has not yet been validated against a standard, it is labelled so rather than presented as though it were.

Availability

  1. Version 1.0.0

    Image, FFT, EDS, EELS, Diffraction and Results rooms, covered by automated unit tests and UI journeys that walk the real app.

  2. Download

    Version 1.0.0 is available as a signed, notarized disk image. Open it and drag macTEM to your Applications folder; it needs nothing else installed.

  3. Open kits

    The kits macTEM is built on are published on GitHub under GPL-3.0, with their parity tables.

Requires macOS 27 or later on Apple silicon. No external runtime, toolchain, or library installation is needed; all dependencies are contained within the application.
