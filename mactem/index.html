The workspace

Capabilities

  • ImagesImaging · Image

    DM3/DM4, Velox EMD, TIFF stacks, MRC and SEM TIFFs at their calibration. Contrast, distances, angles, profiles, particles.

  • FFTImaging · FFT

    The live transform, radial profile, spot d-spacings, a masked inverse and HRTEM filters.

  • DiffractionDiffraction

    Calibrate on a ring or from the file, fit rings, index spots against a standard or a CIF.

  • EBSD patternsEBSD · Patterns

    h5ebsd, EDAX, Oxford and NORDIF scans. Backgrounds, filters, NLPAR, virtual BSE.

  • IndexingEBSD · Indexing

    Hough or dictionary indexing against a master pattern, for one or several phases.

  • Orientation mapsEBSD · Maps

    IPF and phase maps, grains, KAM, GOS, GND, slip, pole figures and ODF.

  • EDSSpectroscopy · EDS

    TEM spectrum images and SEM-EDS from Bruker, EDAX and JEOL. Cliff-Lorimer and zeta quantification, maps, regions.

  • EELSSpectroscopy · EELS

    t/λ maps and edge quantification with uncertainties, 383 edges across 95 elements.

  • ResultsResults

    Every measurement in one place. CSV, MSA, PNG with scale bar, a session report, and the method as JSON.

Provenance and validation

The numerics live in the open macEM kits, each tested against the Python package the field already uses: RosettaSciIO for files, NumPy, SciPy and scikit-image for images, pymatgen for crystallography, HyperSpy and exSpy for spectroscopy. The SEM rooms are ported into the app and checked the same way, against kikuchipy, PyEBSDIndex, orix and DefDAP for EBSD. A result not yet validated against a standard is labelled so.

EELS cross sections are the generalised oscillator strengths of Segger, Guzzinati and Kohl, dft GOS v1.5.0, CC BY 4.0, bundled unchanged.
