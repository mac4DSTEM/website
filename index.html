mac4DSTEM

Four-dimensional STEM analysis, native on Apple silicon.

A macOS application for working with 4D-STEM datasets end to end — built on Metal and MLX so reconstruction runs on the GPU in your laptop rather than on a cluster. Free, and coming to the App Store.

  • Virtual imaging Bright / dark field
  • Orientation mapping ACOM
  • Strain mapping Bragg vectors
  • Phase reconstruction Ptychography
